# Rights verification

Webnovel operates fail closed. Approved statuses are public-domain verified, licence verified, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, and permission verified. `UNVERIFIED`, `RESEARCHING`, `NEEDS_LEGAL_REVIEW`, `RESTRICTED`, and `REMOVED` can never be public.

A publishable edition needs manual approval, jurisdiction, verification method, verification and future review dates, plus `INDEPENDENT_MANUAL_REVIEW` evidence. Licensed editions also need the exact licence name and URL; attribution licences need stored attribution. Provider claims do not satisfy the independent-evidence check.

Review the original work, selected edition, translation, illustrator/editor/contributors, publication country/date, author and translator death years, source terms, and target jurisdiction. Singapore is the initial configured jurisdiction, but the rules file is configuration—not legal advice or an automatic public-domain calculator. Uncertainty means `NEEDS_LEGAL_REVIEW` or `RESTRICTED`.

The daily recheck invalidates due records and re-assesses every published novel. Any failure immediately sets `published=false` and `ads_eligible=false`. Takedown actions can temporarily disable or remove a work immediately. Rights evidence is never part of automated cleanup.
