# Monetization

Novel records separate `ads_eligible` from `monetization_status`: not reviewed, eligible, low value, rights uncertain, content unsafe, or disabled. Legal availability alone is insufficient. Eligibility requires publication, complete content, current rights, sufficient length, useful description/enrichment, quality score, and an explicit operator decision.

Landing pages add cover, overview, genres, themes, setting, length, difficulty, characters, author/publication context, chapters, recommendations, community data, and rights/source provenance where verified. AI filler, fake ratings, fabricated traffic, and mass keyword pages are prohibited.

The readiness checker reports blockers and warnings for domain, HTTPS, publisher setup, development disablement, analytics, CMP, sitemap, robots, `ads.txt`, policy pages, and eligible content. It is a preflight aid, not a guarantee of Google approval.
